Zhunt Pro

Getting your licence key…

One moment while we confirm your checkout with Stripe.

Then, in a terminal on the computer where zhunt runs:

zhunt licence activate     # paste the key when asked
zhunt licence status       # shows Pro and what it includes

The key is checked on your machine, offline. Keep it private: anyone with it can use your Pro plan. When your subscription renews, run zhunt licence renew.
